# WayOfProcessing

> Stripe as a payment system for business — setup and processing from 1.5%

## About

WayOfProcessing helps companies connect Stripe as an international payment system: merchant account, checkout, payment routing, CRM and payouts in EUR, USD, USDT.

WayOfProcessing is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a subsidiary of Stripe, Inc.

## Key facts

- 1100+ Stripe merchant setups for clients
- 7 years of Stripe experience
- $53M+ total client payment volume
- Processing from 1.5%
- 99.9% uptime with payment routing failover
- Free expert consultation
- Languages: Russian (/ru, default), English (/en)
